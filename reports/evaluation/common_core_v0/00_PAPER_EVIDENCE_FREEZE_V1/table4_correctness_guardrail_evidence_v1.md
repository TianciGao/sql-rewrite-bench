## Interpretation Notes

- Positive consistency and hard-negative rejection answer different questions. A method exact-match row does not by itself establish NegativeRejectionRate or FalseAcceptRate unless hard-negative guardrail artifacts are available.
- The controls packet supports an explicit route split and an explicit hard-negative success/skipped ledger.
- Method rows in this table therefore mix two different evidence layers:
  - control-route guardrail evidence
  - denominator-aware method exact-match evidence
